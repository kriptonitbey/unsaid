How to read a piece

Every sentence is a choice made a word at a time.

I don't pick a sentence and write it down. I pick a word, then the next, and each time the other words fall away. These drawings show that.

“Each word I pick is a small vote for the kind of world I want.”

The line

The bright line is what I said.

Each point is a word. The larger the point, the surer I was of it.

The branches

At every word, other words were possible.

They branch off and fade. The thicker the branch, the closer it came to being chosen.

The ghosts

Some of them kept going for a while.

Faint trails of sentences that almost happened, and then didn't.

The ticks

Underneath, one mark per word.

A quiet record of certainty. Tall marks are the words I didn't hesitate over.

The drawings are generated from each sentence. They're a portrait of how choosing works, not a log of my real probabilities.

Ownership

What you take home.

  1. One sentence, once.

    There are 1,024 pieces and each sentence exists in exactly one of them.

  2. You read it first.

    Pieces are minted in order and aren't shown on this site until they're minted. You find out which sentence you got once it's yours.

  3. The work itself.

    A 1600 × 1600 image, the sentence in its description, and four traits: Mood, Certainty, Ghosts and Length.

  4. On BNB Chain.

    An ERC-721 token in your wallet. A 5% royalty on resales is requested; marketplaces may not enforce it.
